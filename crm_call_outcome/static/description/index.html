CRM Call Outcome

Log the result of a call from the lead / opportunity

Adds a Log call button next to the phone number on a lead or opportunity. It opens a popup where you record:

  • Call status — Connected or Voice mail
  • Outcome — Not interested / Right person not in the office / Request to call back
  • Feedback — free notes about the call

Saving posts a formatted summary in the record's chatter. No field on the lead is changed. Uninstall cleanly at any time.
